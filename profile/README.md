# About Morph

Morph installs and operates technical go-to-market systems. We map the market, come up with creative ways into it, deploy the revenue system, and hand it over or continue to operate it.

We serve B2B companies selling into US-based hospitals, health systems, physician groups, or private practices. These markets are challenging to build with complex sales cycles and low outbound effectiveness. We create systems behind your team to help you get at your market in creative ways.

## What we build

These are layers of one system, not separate services.

- **Market intelligence:** account universes, entity resolution, ownership and affiliation, and custom research on markets that don't come clean out of a database.
- **GTM engineering:** targeted outbound systems, signals, enrichment, routing, and suppression.
- **Revenue systems:** CRM implementation and architecture, handoffs, automation, and reporting that connects market data to sellers.
- **AI agents and automation:** research, monitoring, routing, and maintenance that take on the mechanical work while people keep review.
- **Data infrastructure:** models and pipelines that bring market sources, CRM, product, and finance data into one picture.
- **Managed operation:** monitoring, QA, documentation, and extension, so you aren't coordinating a stack of narrow vendors.

## How we work

- **The first request is often a symptom.** We look at the whole motion before we build.
- **Built, documented, observable, and yours.** Nothing is a black box.
- **People keep judgment.** Agents take the repetitive work. Commercial decisions stay with your team.
